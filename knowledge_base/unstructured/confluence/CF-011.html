Print queue stalled troubleshooting

Owner: Rohan Shah; Knowledge ID: KB0017

Symptoms

Print queue stalled

Procedure

Inspect spooler logs, clear failed jobs and run a test print.

Validation

  • Check service health after the change.
  • Record evidence and notify the relevant support group.
